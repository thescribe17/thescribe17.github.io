### Charlie Bone and the Time Twister by Jenny Nimmo

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

I finished reading *Charlie Bone and the Time Twister* at almost one o'clock in the morning.

Something is always happening in this series, exactly as it should be in a good children's book.

The story continues to develop the world introduced in *Midnight for Charlie Bone* and provides another entertaining adventure.

From a writer's point of view, the main weakness is that readers really need to read the books in order.

Someone starting with book two would almost certainly miss important background information.

That isn't a problem for me because I always read series in order.

Like many readers, I find it difficult not to compare the Charlie Bone books with Harry Potter.

They are alike in some respects yet very different in others.

Either way, I feel they are worth the investment of both time and money.
