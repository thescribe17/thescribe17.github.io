### Drowned Wednesday by Garth Nix

**Format:** Book

### My review

Well, what can I say? I've outdone myself and managed to read *Drowned Wednesday* in under two weeks. Not often does that happen.

As I said before, Garth Nix has let his imagination run wild with this series and, in my opinion, it works well.

The story is fast-paced and fun to read. The entertainment value is high.

There were no talking eyebrows in this book, but I survived, as I'm sure most readers will.

Strangely, the very last paragraph didn't quite make sense to me. I suppose it was setting up the next book, *Sir Thursday*, but perhaps I missed something in the storyline.

No matter.

I enjoyed the book and would recommend it to all young-at-heart readers who enjoy wacky stories.
