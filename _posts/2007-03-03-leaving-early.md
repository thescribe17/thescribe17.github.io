### Leaving Early by Bronwyn Donaghy

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

*Leaving Early: Youth Suicide – The Horror, the Heartbreak, the Hope* is exactly what the title suggests.

It places the reader in the middle of three families affected by youth suicide.

The stories of Collin, Jason and Maz are contrasted by cold, hard facts. Emotion is followed by statistics. Personal stories are followed by information.

As the pages turned, I often found myself putting the book aside with tears in my eyes.

This is not an easy book to read.

Published in 1997, some of the information is now dated, especially the statistics, but the message remains important.

I suspect only those directly affected by suicide would consider picking up this book.

That is a shame.

If we can educate parents and children before tragedy strikes, perhaps more lives could be saved.

What struck me most was that the book contains hope as well as grief.

While some stories ended in tragedy, another showed that intervention, communication and support can save a life.

I began reading this book as research, but finished it having learned far more about grief, suicide and human resilience.

This book should be read by everyone, not only by families who have lost a loved one.
