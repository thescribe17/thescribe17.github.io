### Shadows by John Saul

**Format:** Audiobook

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

*Shadows* is the first audiobook I've listened to.

I thought I would have problems with my mind wandering, but the story was so intriguing that I found myself completely absorbed in what was happening.

The story centres on a school for genius children and explores issues such as isolation, teasing, boredom, lack of friendship and suicidal thoughts.

Life at the new school initially appears normal and accepting, but things gradually become sinister.

One section dealing with suicide made me quite angry. As someone who has lost a son to suicide, I felt the author lacked genuine understanding of the subject. Yet alongside those frustrations were ideas and observations that I found insightful.

Once the story moved beyond that topic, I found myself enjoying it more.

The technical aspects sounded convincing and I became increasingly eager to discover how the story would end.

The ending was satisfying and avoided a direction that I had feared the author might take.

For my first audiobook experience, I think I chose well. I enjoyed it and would happily try another audiobook in the future.
