**My Review:**

You would have noticed by the sidebar that I've been reading The Little Country by Charles de Lint. Last night I finished it.

<p>What did I think?</p>

<p>It started out really well, very interesting. There are two stories running parallel with each other...</p>

<p>The pros for the book was that the author used his imagination and touched on things that I've thought about...</p>

<p>The cons were that there were several scenes that I felt were there for shock purposes...</p>

<p>Overall, this was a good read...</p>
