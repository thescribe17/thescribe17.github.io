### Perfect Victim by Elizabeth Southall and Megan Norris

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

*Perfect Victim* is the true story of the disappearance and murder of fifteen-year-old Rachel Barber.

The book follows two parallel narratives: the grief and anguish of Rachel's family, and the investigation and court proceedings that followed.

Because of the nature of the subject matter, I don't feel it is appropriate to discuss this book in the same way I would a novel.

Instead, what affected me most was the exploration of grief.

The loss of a child through murder and the loss of a child through suicide are very different experiences, yet they also share many similarities.

Questions remain unanswered.

Guilt lingers.

Lives are changed forever.

Reading Elizabeth's words brought me to tears.

The emotions felt genuine and deeply personal.

What resonated most strongly was her determination to increase understanding and awareness among those who work with grieving families.

The book is heartbreaking, educational and profoundly moving.

It is a difficult read, but an important one.
