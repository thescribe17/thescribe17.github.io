### Deep Water by Pamela Freeman

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

*Deep Water* is the second book in *The Castings Trilogy*.

What impressed me most was how effectively the author tied every part of the story together.

Even the smaller character stories played important roles in the overall narrative.

As a reader, I enjoyed the depth of the characters and understanding what made them the people they were.

Even characters I disliked often earned my sympathy once their backstories were revealed.

As a writer, I was fascinated by the way flashbacks and side stories were used.

The flow of the writing felt effortless. Character names, locations and worldbuilding never became obstacles to the reading experience.

There were also no lengthy descriptions or weather reports, both of which I generally dislike.

My only criticism is that the trilogy tells one continuous story, meaning readers must remember events between books.

Even so, I enjoyed both books immensely and highly recommend them.
