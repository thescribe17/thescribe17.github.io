### The Hobbit by J. R. R. Tolkien

**Format:** Audiobook

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

Although I've always been an avid reader, I wasn't introduced to Tolkien until the *Lord of the Rings* films were released.

I enjoyed the films immensely but found the books dull in comparison. It's unusual for me to enjoy a film adaptation more than the books, but in this case I certainly did.

As a result, I vowed never to read another Tolkien book.

Then G brought home an audiobook version of *The Hobbit* from the library.

Eventually I found time to listen to it during my train commute.

To my surprise, I became completely engrossed in the story. At one point I nearly missed my stop, something that had never happened before.

The narrator was excellent. Different voices and accents were used for characters, and music and sound effects helped establish mood and atmosphere.

I'm glad I took the time to listen to this story. It far exceeded my expectations.

Highly recommended.
