## Moonlight

**Rating:** 4 of 5 stars

The first book surprised me.

The cover did nothing for me, but the blurb caught my attention and I decided to give it a chance.

I'm glad I did.

Yes, there are cheesy moments. Yes, there are scenes that made me roll my eyes. However, the story is easy to read, absorbing and entertaining.

I particularly enjoyed the setting and the idea of werewolves being portrayed as the good guys rather than the monsters.

The characters, especially Kayla and Lucas, carried the story well.

---

## Full Moon

**Rating:** 3 of 5 stars

The second book continues the story from a different character's perspective.

It took me some time to stop focusing on Kayla and Lucas and adjust to Lindsey's story.

Once I did, I found myself drawn back into the world.

The book expands the history of the Dark Guardians and develops the larger conflicts introduced in book one.

While I still enjoyed it, the freshness of the first novel had faded, which lowered my enjoyment slightly.

---

## Dark of the Moon

**Rating:** 3.5 of 5 stars

This time the story focuses on Brittany.

Because of the circumstances she faces, I found her easier to relate to than Lindsey.

The emotional themes felt stronger and more realistic in this instalment.

My biggest criticism was how quickly one romantic storyline shifted direction.

Even so, I found this book more engaging than the second.

The conclusion also resolved the larger Bio-Chrome storyline in a satisfying manner.

### Overall

If you enjoy romance, paranormal fiction and easy-to-read stories, I recommend the series.

The first book remains my favourite, but all three provided enjoyable reading.
