### Midnight for Charlie Bone by Jenny Nimmo

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

I originally bought this book because I thought it might be similar to *Harry Potter*.

While there are surface similarities, such as a young boy with a special ability and a school for gifted children, the story is really quite different.

Once I stopped comparing it to Harry Potter and allowed it to stand on its own, I enjoyed it much more.

The book drew me in from the beginning.

I loved the characters and the setting, and I appreciated how the author allowed problems to be partially resolved while leaving room for future growth.

Although this is the first book in a series, it felt complete enough to stand on its own.

The immediate conflict was resolved, yet there was still a sense that more adventures lay ahead.

Recommendation: definitely yes. Read this book.
