### Keeper of the Winds by Jenna Solitaire

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

*Keeper of the Winds* is the first book in the *Daughter of Destiny* series.

From a writer's perspective, two things stood out to me.

The first was positive. I enjoyed the author's style and voice. It felt comfortable and easy to read, which allowed me to settle into the story and enjoy the suspense.

I also liked the short teaser scenes at the beginning of chapters. They worked well and encouraged me to keep reading.

The second point relates to the main character.

While the supporting characters felt believable, I struggled with the protagonist. Her constant refusal to accept what was happening around her eventually became frustrating and, at times, unrealistic.

I also found myself occasionally confused about the passage of time between scenes.

Despite those issues, the overall story remained interesting enough that I wanted to know where everything was heading.

I think readers of fantasy and young adult fiction will enjoy this book.
