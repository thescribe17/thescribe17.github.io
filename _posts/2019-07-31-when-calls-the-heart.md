### When Calls the Heart by Janette Oke

**Format:** eBook

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### Blurb

Nothing in her cultured East Coast upbringing prepared Elizabeth for a teaching position on the Canadian frontier.

Yet despite the constant hardships, she loves the children in her care.

Determined to do the best job she can, Elizabeth is surprised to find her heart softening towards a certain member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

### My review

Firstly, I must state that I watched the Canadian television series.

I watched every episode and fell in love with Elizabeth and Jack. I was firmly on Team Jack.

When I discovered the series was based on books, naturally I set out to discover if my local library had any.

To my delight, I found the first four ebooks available for borrowing.

I was a little put out that Jack was replaced with Wynn and that much of the television series was not to be found in this first book.

Yet, once I let go of what I expected to read and accepted this new storyline, I also fell in love with the Elizabeth and Wynn romance.

In my opinion, the book is grittier and portrays a truer account of how people lived and interacted in those days.

Life would have been difficult at best.

I do not regret reading the book and would recommend it to anyone.

100% recommended.
