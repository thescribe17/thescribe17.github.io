### Rhett Butler's People by Donald McCaig

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 2 of 5 stars

### Blurb

An authorised continuation of the world of *Gone with the Wind*, following Rhett Butler's life, family and relationships through his own perspective.

### My review

As a fan of *Gone with the Wind* and its sequel *Scarlett*, I knew I had to read this book.

For several reasons, I was disappointed.

The title is accurate, but the story focuses less on Rhett and Scarlett than I expected.

Much of the novel explores other people in Rhett's life and their stories.

I also found the American Civil War aspects dominated the narrative more than I would have liked.

War stories have never really appealed to me.

That said, the writing itself was quite readable and several storylines were genuinely absorbing.

The characters were well developed and the settings felt authentic.

My biggest disappointment came from the way the novel handled events after *Gone with the Wind*.

As someone who enjoyed the previously authorised sequel *Scarlett*, I struggled with the decision to ignore that continuation completely.

Overall, the book was good, but it never captured my imagination in the same way its predecessors did.
