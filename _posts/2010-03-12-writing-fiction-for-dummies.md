### Writing Fiction for Dummies by Randy Ingermanson and Peter Economy

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3.5 of 5 stars

### My review

*Writing Fiction for Dummies* is an excellent resource for new writers.

It covers the basics of writing fiction, from planning stories through to preparing work for publication.

As someone with writing experience already, I didn't get as much from the book as a complete beginner would.

However, I still found it valuable.

I purchased it for three reasons:

1. One of the co-authors is Randy Ingermanson, creator of the Snowflake Method.
2. I wanted inspiration and motivation for my own projects.
3. I wanted guidance on writing book proposals.

The proposal section alone justified the purchase.

I learned far more than I expected and even created a proposal template for future projects.

Perhaps the greatest benefit was the reminder that persistence and determination are the keys to moving forward as a writer.

By the time I finished reading, I felt enthusiastic about my next project.

And that's never a bad thing.
