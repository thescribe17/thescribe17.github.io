### For One More Day by Mitch Albom

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

Last weekend I was in the library and a book jumped off the shelf and slapped me in the face.

The cover of *For One More Day* is as plain as they come. In fact, if the book had been named anything else I think I would have thrown it back onto the shelf and kept walking.

Being a bereaved mother, the title spoke to me in volumes. Without reading anything more than those four words, I knew I wanted to read the book.

And my gut instinct was right.

As children, we often think our parents are wrong. We don't understand why they refuse to let us do the things we want to do. We shy away from them and say things that hurt them.

As adults, we still don't fully understand them.

Then we become parents ourselves and suddenly everything falls into place.

We finally see the sacrifices our parents made. We finally see the mistakes they made and the reasons behind them.

If you are a parent now, can you imagine what it would be like to be given the chance to go back and right a wrong with your mother, especially if she is dead?

*For One More Day* gives a son that opportunity.

Charley's life is in ruins and he wants to end his life, but instead he gets to spend one more day with his mother.

He learns things about his family he never knew. He learns things about his mother. He learns things about himself.

This story is written in a way that feels private and deeply personal. It had me in tears.

Every family has its secrets. Some children never learn the reasons for important decisions made by their parents.

If we had the chance to spend one more day with a lost relative, what would we learn? What would we say?

I highly recommend *For One More Day* to anyone with a heart.

You will not regret the time spent within this world.
