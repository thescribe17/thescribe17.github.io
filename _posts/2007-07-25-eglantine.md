### Eglantine by Catherine Jinks

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

While reading *Eglantine*, I kept experiencing a strange sense of familiarity.

The characters, the setting and the events all felt vaguely familiar.

Only at the end did I realise why.

I had actually read the book before and completely forgotten.

Despite that revelation, I enjoyed revisiting the story.

*Eglantine* is a paranormal adventure aimed at readers aged eight to twelve.

The supernatural storyline is combined with information about anorexia, and I thought the author handled that balance very well.

Some younger readers may find a few moments slightly scary, but the overall tone remains light-hearted and approachable.

A worthwhile read for younger readers.
