### The Love Knot by Elizabeth Chadwick

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

Although it took me longer than usual to read this book, there was a good reason for it.

The author uses a writing style that fits the twelfth-century setting, which meant reading more slowly in order to absorb the language and story.

Far from being a hindrance, it gave the story authenticity.

I have always enjoyed a good romance story, and this was one of them.

A true romance has a real story behind it and characters you would want to befriend in real life.

The thing I especially liked about this book was the history. I've always had an interest in castles, knights and medieval life.

The details were clearly well researched and that helped me settle into the story.

The author showed the dirt, hardship, pain and death, but also how people found contentment with what little they had.

I appreciated that.

*The Love Knot* is a story worth reading. I recommend it and I'll be keeping an eye open for more books by this author.
