### When Hope Springs New by Janette Oke

**Format:** eBook

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### Blurb

Leaving behind their dear friends in Beaver Creek, Elizabeth and Wynn take over an even more primitive RCMP outpost in the Canadian Northwest.

Elizabeth finds herself totally isolated when the local Indian women are afraid to communicate with her.

Would they be able to survive the challenges ahead?

### My review

The fourth book in the series takes Elizabeth and Wynn away from Beaver Creek and even further north.

They end up living in an Indian village in a shack with a dirt floor.

The Indians in the village are superstitious and will not talk to Elizabeth. However, being used to having a Mountie around, they are civil with Wynn.

Some of the storyline I felt was far-fetched. Other sections I felt were too convenient.

Overall, I continued to keep reading because of my dedication to the couple. I wanted to see where their story would end up.

I felt satisfied with where this book ended and made the decision not to read the last two books in the series.

The first book was great. The second very good. The third and fourth were not bad, but started to dull a little.

Having said that, I still recommend them.

I'm glad I read them.
