### The Testaments by Margaret Atwood

**Format:** Audiobook

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

This is book two in *The Handmaid's Tale* series.

Book one had a voice that drew me in and kept me spellbound. Book two has a different voice, which I readily accepted because different characters tell the story.

I read some bad reviews before reading book two. Terrible reviews.

However, I must disagree with them.

*The Testaments* revisits some well-known characters and introduces new ones. It provides more backstory and fills in blanks left at the end of book one.

For me, the book beckoned me back to it during the hours that I could not return to Gilead.

Surprisingly, I identified with the story because of what was happening in the world at the time.

Book one felt as if we were seeing Gilead from the inside. It left me feeling oppressed and worthless. Offred had no hope and surrounded the reader with that same feeling.

However, book two felt as if it saw Gilead from the outside looking in.

We see how a person can appear mean and nasty but be fighting the fight behind closed doors. We witness officials' weaknesses and their enemies.

And, like every oppressive era, we discover brave people willing to stand and fight.

What I'm trying to say is that book one takes hope away and book two gives it back.

Personally, I think both books are brilliant, and I highly recommend them.
