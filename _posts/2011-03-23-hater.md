### Hater by David Moody

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### Blurb

Society is rocked by a sudden increase in violent attacks by ordinary people.

Without warning, rational and self-controlled individuals become remorseless killers.

As the violence spreads, everyone becomes a potential victim... or a potential Hater.

### My review

*Hater* is the sort of book you pick up and, before you realise it, several hours have passed.

The writing style is simple and straightforward, which makes it easy to become immersed in the story.

The opening scene grabbed my attention immediately.

The main character felt completely real. I could relate to his frustrations with work, family pressures and the feeling that life could be doing better.

As the violence escalated, so did the tension.

I understood his fear and his determination to protect his family at all costs.

The novel constantly made me ask difficult questions.

What would I do in the same situation?

Would I be willing to kill to protect those I love?

The story is fast-paced, thought-provoking and surprisingly relatable despite its extreme premise.

My only criticism is that the explanation behind the events felt vague. I suspect later books in the trilogy may address this more fully.

Some readers call it zombie-like fiction, but there are no undead here.

Just people.

And that makes it all the more unsettling.
