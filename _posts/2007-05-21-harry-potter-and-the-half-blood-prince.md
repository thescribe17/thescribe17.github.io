### Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince by J. K. Rowling

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

I finished reading *Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince* a few weeks ago but only just found the time to write a review.

With more than six hundred pages, it is far longer than the books I usually read these days. Yet the story kept me intrigued from beginning to end.

The well-known characters and strong plot carried me effortlessly from chapter to chapter.

What stood out most was the author's ability to keep readers questioning motives and loyalties.

In particular, I found myself constantly wondering about Snape and which side he was really on.

I was also impressed by how much the series had matured. The characters felt older, the stakes felt higher, and the sense that everything was leading towards a major conclusion was unmistakable.

While there were a few story decisions I personally questioned, I still found the book compelling and couldn't wait for the final instalment.

Overall, an excellent lead-in to the final book of the series.
