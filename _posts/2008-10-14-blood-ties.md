### Blood Ties by Pamela Freeman

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

<p>The story is well written - smooth and interesting. The characters are not perfect people, which make them realistic, and they are likable and well rounded. And the world is believable yet enchanting.</p>

<p>I was especially impressed with the flow of the story. It is so easy to read, which makes it almost impossible to put down. From what I’ve heard, the second book (Deep Water) is quite the page turner so I’m looking forward to reading it soon. Another unique quality of the book is what I call the mini-stories of the minor characters. They give the story depth and allow the reader to view storylines from different perspectives, which is brilliant.</p>

<p>In all honesty, Blood Ties is the best adult novel I’ve read in a while. I highly recommend it.</p>
