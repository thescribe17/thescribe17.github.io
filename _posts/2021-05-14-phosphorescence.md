### Phosphorescence by Julia Baird

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

This book is completely different from my usual reading material.

I received it as a gift and decided to give it a try without really knowing what to expect.

What I found was not a novel at all, but an inspirational and reflective exploration of life, wonder and resilience.

Oddly enough, the book begins with jellyfish.

That surprised me.

It also captivated me.

The book encourages readers to slow down, appreciate nature and find contentment in the smaller aspects of life.

Different readers will undoubtedly take different lessons from its pages.

For me, it offered an opportunity to view the world through someone else's eyes and to appreciate the healing power of nature and human connection.

A couple of chapters felt longer than necessary and lost my attention.

Apart from those sections, however, I enjoyed the book immensely.
