### Blood Ties by Pamela Freeman

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

*Blood Ties* is the first book in *The Castings Trilogy* and the first adult novel by Pamela Freeman.

Having enjoyed several of her children's books, I had high hopes.

I wasn't disappointed.

The story is smooth, engaging and very easy to read.

The characters are realistic and well rounded, while the world feels believable and enchanting.

What impressed me most were the mini-stories woven throughout the novel.

These side stories add depth and allow readers to experience events from different perspectives.

They enrich the entire narrative.

This is the best adult fantasy novel I had read in quite some time when I first encountered it.

Highly recommended.
