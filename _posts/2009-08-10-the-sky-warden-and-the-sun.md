### The Sky Warden and the Sun by Sean Williams

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

This is the second book of *The Change* trilogy.

Sal and Shilly continue their adventure after the events of the first book.

In the first book they were children. In this one we see their forced growth as they struggle with decisions that many adults would have trouble making.

Again, the story isn't fast-paced, but the characters, setting and plot are not lacking.

I was impressed by the sense of realism and felt as if I knew the two main characters extremely well.

The other thing that impressed me was that this middle volume didn't sag at all, something that often happens in trilogies.

Every scene felt important.

This book is highly recommended and I was eager to read the final volume.
