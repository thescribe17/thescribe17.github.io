**Your privacy is important to me.**

This website is intended as a personal author website and does not collect personal information beyond what visitors voluntarily provide through the contact form.

## Contact Forms

Any information you provide through the contact form is supplied voluntarily and will only be used to respond to your enquiry. Once I have replied, your email and any personal information provided will be deleted.

I do not maintain mailing lists, store personal information, or sell, share, or provide your details to any third party.

## Book Review Requests

If you contact me regarding a book review, any information you provide will only be used to determine whether your book is suitable for review.

Should I choose to review your book, the following information may be included in the review:

- Author name
- Book title
- Book cover
- Book blurb
- My review and rating

Reviews may be published on:

- This website
- Goodreads
- LibraryThing

Please note that I have no control over how third-party websites handle information once a review has been published on their platforms.

After a review has been published, any information supplied during the review request process will be permanently deleted from my records. Apart from the published review itself, I do not retain copies of the information submitted and will never sell or share it with third parties.

## Cookies and Analytics

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and other third-party services may use cookies or collect technical information as part of providing their services.

I do not use analytics software to identify visitors, track their location, or collect information about how individuals use this website.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the [contact form](/contact/) on this website.
