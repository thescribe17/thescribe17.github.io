One of my regular grammar traps is knowing when to use **passed** and when to use **past**. I  know the difference when I stop and think about it, but when I'm writing quickly, I sometimes find myself pausing and asking, "Is it passed or past?"

To save myself time, and another trip to a dictionary or grammar website, here's a quick reference guide. Hopefully it helps you too.

### Passed

**Passed** is a verb. Something is happening.

Examples:

- She **passed** the test.
- The car **passed** me.
- Time **passed** quickly.

### Past

**Past** usually refers to time, position, or direction.

Examples:

- We walked **past** the library.
- Learn from the **past**.
- It is half **past** three.

### Quick Tip

If an action is taking place, use **passed**.

If you're referring to time, location, or history, use **past**.

### Common Mistakes

❌ I walked **passed** the shop.

✅ I walked **past** the shop.

❌ The weeks **past** quickly.

✅ The weeks **passed** quickly.
