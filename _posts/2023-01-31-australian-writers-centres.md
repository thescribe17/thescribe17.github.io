<p>Your local writers' centre can be a useful resource. Not only can you find like minded people to talk to (if you live close enough to visit in person), but most centres also have a library, an assortment of workshops, regular talks by published writers and they can even provide advice on contracts, agents, and publishers.</p>

<p>Becoming a member means that you have something you can add to your writer's resume too (which never goes astray).</p>

<p>Below, you will find links to a number of Australian centres, with a short blurb from the appropriate website.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.actwriters.org.au/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ACT Writers Centre</a><br>The ACT Writers Centre has a Meeting Room available free for use by members and a computer, printer, fax and photocopier available for use by members. We have a growing library of books about writing and by writers. We also sell books by members on consignment. The noticeboards are full of information about publishing, competitions, writers' rights, and writing courses.</p>

<p><a href="https://wordsoutwest.westerndownslibraries.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Central West Writers' Centre</a><br>The Central West Writers' Centre provides development and promotion services for literary activity in rural Australia in the Central West of New South Wales.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.nswwriterscentre.org.au/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">NSW Writers' Centre</a><br>The Centre offers literary resources and professional information to established and aspiring writers of all kinds. It provides a spacious venue for events such as book launches, readings, literary evenings and lectures as well as meeting spaces for writers' groups and literary organisations.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.ntwriters.com.au/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">NT Writers' Centre</a><br>The Centre offers a range of activities and services for writers including workshops, literary events, manuscript appraisal, a regular newsletter, special projects and an annual writers' festival.</p>

<p><a href="https://queenslandwriters.org.au/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Queensland Writers' Centre</a><br>Provides writing tips and resources, advice on handling rejection and rates of pay, details for workshops, seminars, competitions and much more.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.sawriters.on.net/">SA Writers' Centre</a><br>The Centre acts as a resource centre for writers of all ages and experiences. They focus on writing activities and work with a wide range of organisations to promote and encourage writers and literature in society.</p>

<p><a href="https://taswriters.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Tasmanian Writers' Centre</a><br>The Tasmanian Writers' Centre supports numerous initiatives that promote Tasmanian's appreciation of literature. These include workshops, residencies and mentorships for Tasmanian writers, as well as providing professional advice to TWC members.</p>

<p><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="http://www.vwc.org.au/" target="_blank">Victorian Writers' Centre</a><br>The Victorian Writers' Centre is dedicated to nurturing and promoting the diverse writing culture in Victoria. As the leading provider of information, resources and skills development, the VWC connects and supports writers and writing within the broader communities throughout Victoria.</p>
