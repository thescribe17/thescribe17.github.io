### Clockwork by Philip Pullman

**Format:** Book

### Blurb

Fritz the writer spins a tale to cheer up Karl, the apprentice clockmaker.

Unfortunately, the story begins to come true.

### My review

I started and finished *Clockwork* in a single day.

The book is only eighty-one pages long, so that achievement sounds more impressive than it really is.

This is very much a traditional fairy-tale style story.

The opening immediately told me not to take things too seriously, and I think that helped me enjoy it.

One surprising aspect was the amount of macabre material included in a children's book.

Characters are injured, threatened and placed in situations that are quite dark.

Yet the author handles these moments carefully enough that they remain suitable for younger readers.

Was it the best children's book I've ever read?

No.

But it was an enjoyable distraction for a cold afternoon.
