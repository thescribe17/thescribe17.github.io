### The Starthorn Tree by Kate Forsyth

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

I found this book difficult to get into because of the dialogue and unusual names.

Once I adjusted to the language, however, the story improved greatly.

The story is a classic quest.

Five children from very different backgrounds are brought together for a common cause.

There are soldiers, magical creatures, danger, humour, sadness and even a touch of romance.

The poor children are faced with horrors that would curl your toes and must overcome differences that adults often fail to overcome.

Apart from the unsettled beginning, I found this book to be an interesting and exciting read.

Recommended.
