I'm delighted to announce that I'm currently working on the next book in my **Cat and Mouse Adventure** series for younger readers.

### 👻 Ghost at the Cemetery, #2

Cat and Mouse are back for another mystery, and this time their investigation takes them somewhere that has sparked rumours, whispers, and more than a few curious glances... the local cemetery.

When strange events begin unsettling the community, Cat and Mouse find themselves drawn into a puzzling new case. As they follow the clues, they soon discover that things are not always what they seem. The deeper they dig, the more secrets they uncover, leading to an adventure filled with mystery, surprises, and unexpected twists.

While I don't want to give away too much just yet, I can promise another exciting adventure for young readers who enjoy mysteries, clues to solve, and memorable characters.

### A Return to Adventure

Writing the Cat and Mouse Adventures has been a wonderful journey, and I'm excited to spend more time with these characters as they tackle their latest challenge. If you enjoyed the first book in the series, I'm looking forward to sharing this next adventure with you.
