### Promise Me Tomorrow by Lori Wick

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

*Promise Me Tomorrow* is a Christian romance and, despite its predictability, I loved it.

The religious content was generally handled well, aside from one section that wasn't for me.

At its heart, this is a story about a man and woman overcoming obstacles in order to be together.

It's sweet, sentimental and completely predictable.

Yet that is precisely why I enjoyed it.

Reading it reminded me why I originally fell in love with romance stories.

I adore stories built around attraction, longing, admiration and all the small moments that make relationships meaningful.

This book reminded me that sometimes we should enjoy the stories we genuinely love, not the stories we think we're supposed to love.

Recommended for readers with a tender heart.
