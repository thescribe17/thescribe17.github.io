### Insomnia by Stephen King

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 1 of 5 stars

### My review

My parents always told me, "If you can't say something nice, don't say anything at all."

Unfortunately, I'm about to break that rule.

I didn't like *Insomnia*.

The concept was excellent, but I felt the story wandered far too much.

The author seemed to waffle on and on until I lost all connection with both the characters and the plot.

Eventually I simply stopped caring what happened to anyone.

I found myself skimming paragraphs just to reach the end faster.

I've read other Stephen King novels and enjoyed them a great deal.

Not this one.

For me, it was a total waste of time.
