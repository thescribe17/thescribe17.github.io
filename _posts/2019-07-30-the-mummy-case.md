### The Mummy Case by Elizabeth Peters

**Format:** Audiobook

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### Blurb

Join our plucky Victorian Egyptologist, together with her devastatingly handsome and brilliant husband Radcliffe, in another exciting escapade.

The irascible husband of Victorian Egyptologist Amelia Peabody is living up to his reputation as "The Father of Curses".

Denied permission to dig at the pyramids of Dahshoor, Emerson is awarded instead the pyramids of Mazghunah. But then a murder in Cairo changes everything.

### My review

Book three of the series.

Enter Ramses, Amelia and Emerson's son.

He's a totally different character who blends nicely with his mum and dad's personalities. A chip off the old block? Not sure about that, but he seems to be a quieter version of his parents.

However, his knowledge is great and he tends to follow trouble, or perhaps trouble follows him.

I listened to the first three books in the series between May and June 2019 and I'm only just getting around to writing the review.

I notice I gave this one three stars. Seems a bit nasty of me now, but I must have had good reasons at the time, although I can't remember them now.

Maybe listening to three in a row was a bit much.

The mystery side of the book is where I felt it was let down. I guess I noticed a bit of a formula happening. That's what happens when you read them one after the other and within quick succession.

Anyway, the formula made the mystery predictable.

Don't get me wrong, the book is good. The characters are the main drawcard. I still love them to bits.

And I would still recommend the series to avid readers.
