### Dragon Keeper by Robin Hobb

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

The book started out slowly and was even a little boring. I almost put it aside unfinished.

However, Robin Hobb is known, at least to me, for slow beginnings and brilliant endings, so I persevered.

Once the characters and setting were established, the pace picked up and I found myself thoroughly immersed in this world of dragons.

Thymara, touched by the Rain Wilds and born with claws and scales, should have been disposed of at birth. Alise dreams of studying dragons despite her marriage. Leftrin, captain of a liveship, will do anything for money and love.

The three main characters are complemented by a strong cast of secondary characters. Their stories feel complex and real.

In my opinion, what the author lacks in the ability to get straight into the story, she makes up for in character and world development.

This is a brilliant book once you get past the first fifty pages.

However, the story ended without resolution. Right in the middle of the tension, it simply finished and deferred everything to book two.

That was a disappointment.
