If you've visited this website before or browsed through the archives, you'll know that book reviews are a regular feature here. Reading has always been one of my favourite hobbies, and I generally review every book I read.

To help support independent authors and publishers, I am pleased to announce that I am currently accepting review requests.

If you're looking for an honest review of your book, I'd love to hear from you.

## Free Book Reviews for Indie Authors and Publishers

There is no charge for this service.

The only thing I ask is that you provide a review copy of your book. I currently accept review copies in the following formats:

- Paperback
- Audiobook
- EPUB
- MOBI

Unfortunately, I do not accept PDF copies.

---

## What I'm Looking For

### Children's and Young Adult Fiction

- Adventure
- Fantasy
- Mystery

### Adult Fiction

- Fantasy
- Mystery
- Romance
- Feel-good fiction

I enjoy stories with memorable characters, engaging plots, and strong storytelling that keeps readers turning pages.

---

## What I'm Not Looking For

At this time, I do not accept:

- Erotica
- Horror
- Non-fiction
- Poetry

If your book falls outside these categories but you think it may be a good fit for me or my audience, please use the contact form and tell me a little about it.

---

## Please Read Before Submitting

Before submitting your book, please keep the following in mind:

- Acceptance of a review copy does not guarantee a review.
- I reserve the right to decline any review request.
- I may choose not to finish a book that does not hold my interest.
- I reserve the right not to publish a review for any book I accept.
- Any review that is published will be completely honest.

My goal is to help readers discover great books while providing authors with thoughtful and genuine feedback.
