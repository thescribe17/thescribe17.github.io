### Full Circle by Pamela Freeman

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

*Full Circle* is the third book in a fantasy trilogy and the conclusion to a story I had been waiting years to finish.

Because of the long gap between books, I struggled at first to remember some details and characters. After refreshing my memory, however, I settled into the story completely.

I'm glad I made the effort.

*Full Circle* is a brilliant conclusion.

The story picks up where the previous book left off and moves smoothly towards its climax.

I especially enjoyed the multiple viewpoints and the way events changed meaning when seen through different eyes. It felt natural and cleverly done.

As the story moved towards its conclusion, I became convinced I knew how everything would unfold.

I was wrong.

The author surprised me completely.

The ending was sad, but right.

I felt tears welling up yet remained completely satisfied with the outcome.

This trilogy is highly recommended.
