### The Da Vinci Code by Dan Brown

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 2 of 5 stars

### My review

I saw the movie when it first came out, but couldn't remember much of the storyline.

Despite what people say, I felt the storyline was good and plausible. It was well developed and, for the most part, fast-paced.

Whether the details were factual or not didn't concern me because the book is fiction. The author made me believe they could be true, and that is all that matters.

The characters were realistic, although I felt they talked too much during crucial life-and-death situations.

But...

What let this book down for me was the overly long non-fiction aspect of the story.

I was reading a novel and didn't want lengthy history lessons thrown at me every few pages.

Some sections were pages long and I found that more than a little annoying. In fact, after the first hundred pages or so, I stopped reading those sections.

Without the research sections, this book would have been a fast-paced page-turner.

It's a shame because I think the storyline on its own would have been great.
