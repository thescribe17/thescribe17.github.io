### Dragonfly by John Farris

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 2 of 5 stars

### My review

Last week I finished reading *Dragonfly*, but I didn't have time to write a review until now.

On the front cover of the book Stephen King is quoted as saying that nobody writes horror better than John Farris. A blurb like this gives the reader high expectations, so I was disappointed to discover that the book isn't horror at all.

Despite what you may have been told elsewhere, *Dragonfly* is not a horror story. It is a mystery romance.

The opening scene grabbed my attention and the following scenes kept me interested. The story and characters are well defined and the author's writing style is easy to read.

My only real grievance with this book was that I felt it was much longer than necessary. The final quarter of the book could have been condensed considerably.

I eventually reached a stage where I no longer cared and simply wanted the story to end.

Despite that criticism, the book was enjoyable and I would try reading something else written by the author.
