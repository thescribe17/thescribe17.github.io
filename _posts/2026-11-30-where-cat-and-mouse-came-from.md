One of the questions I occasionally receive is how Cat and Mouse got their names.

The answer is actually quite simple.

The twins are named Catrice and Angus Mouser. It didn't take long for friends and classmates to shorten those names to Cat and Mouse.

What interests me more, however, is how differently the twins react to the nicknames.

Cat embraces hers completely. In fact, she much prefers Cat to Catrice.

Mouse, on the other hand, has a more complicated relationship with his nickname. Like many children, he doesn't always enjoy being labelled by others. Over time, however, he learns that sometimes accepting who you are can be easier than fighting against it.

Another fun detail is Mrs Gilbert.

Readers may have noticed that Mrs Gilbert insists on using the twins' full names. She calls them Angus and Catrice, even when nobody else does. This tiny detail often creates entertaining moments and helps establish the unique relationship she has with her students.

While Cat and Mouse may share the same family, they approach the world very differently. Cat tends to leap straight into a mystery, while Mouse prefers to stop, think, and consider what might happen next.

Together, those differences help make them stronger than they would be on their own.
