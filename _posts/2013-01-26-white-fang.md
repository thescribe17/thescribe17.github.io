### White Fang by Jack London

**Format:** eBook

**My rating:** 4.5 of 5 stars

### Blurb

He was three-quarters wolf and all fury.

Born in a cave during famine in the frozen Arctic, White Fang grows up in a world where only the swift, the strong and the cunning survive.

Only one man has the courage to offer the killer a new life.

### My review

I first read this book when I was a young teenager.

I remember crying then.

I didn't cry this time around, but the actions in this book did strike a chord with me.

I really do detest cruelty to animals, and the cruelty in this book is paramount.

White Fang is a product of his past.

He has been taught to hate. He has been taught to survive at any measure.

He is vicious. He is a killer.

Yet he is these things because he has to be. His other choice is to be the weak link and die.
