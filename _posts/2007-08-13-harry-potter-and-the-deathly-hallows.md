### Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows by J. K. Rowling

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

My eyes were heavy. My mind was vague.

I was trying to function normally after staying up far too late finishing *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows*.

I had a choice: stop reading near the beginning of the climax and spend the next day wondering what happened, or keep going.

I kept reading.

It was worth it.

While young readers were growing up with Harry Potter, J. K. Rowling was growing as a writer.

As a writer myself, I learned a lot from reading the series.

I especially admire her ability to weave intricate threads together, drop clues throughout seven books, and then pull everything together in a satisfying conclusion.

She did a marvellous job.

Now that I've finished the series, I find myself looking at my own children's writing and thinking I need to put more into it. More imagination. More excitement. More time.

I think that's called inspiration.

And now I need someone to talk to about the book because I desperately want to discuss the details.
