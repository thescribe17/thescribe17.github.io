### Assassin's Apprentice by Robin Hobb

**Format:** Book

### My review

Last night I finished reading *Assassin's Apprentice* and came away with mixed feelings.

There were things I loved and things I disliked.

The story follows Fitz, a royal bastard abandoned at court and eventually apprenticed to the king's assassin.

The characters and plot are beautifully woven together.

Robin Hobb's use of first person works exceptionally well and allowed me to connect deeply with Fitz.

I felt his loneliness, pain and struggles.

There were moments that genuinely tugged at the heart.

The main thing I disliked was the historical information provided at the beginning of chapters.

I found these sections distracting and eventually stopped reading them.

There were also places where descriptions went on for too long.

Yet none of those criticisms were enough to ruin the experience.

The story and characters felt so real that I quickly forgave the parts I didn't enjoy.

This is the first book in a trilogy, but it also functions as a satisfying standalone novel.

Highly recommended.
