### The Storm Weaver and the Sand by Sean Williams

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

This is the third and final book of *The Change* trilogy.

The book was riveting from the first page.

I didn't want to put it down and, when I had to, I was eager to return to the story.

As the final volume, all the loose ends were woven together beautifully.

The characters, setting and plot remained exceptional.

I especially admired how smoothly every storyline came together.

The conflicts were realistic and easy to relate to.

I also appreciated the messages contained within the trilogy, particularly the idea that our future is not set in stone and can be changed by the decisions we make.

When writing reviews I try to point out positive and negative aspects, but with this trilogy I struggle to find anything truly negative.

Highly recommended.
