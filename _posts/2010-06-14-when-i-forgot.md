### When I Forgot by Elina Hirvonen

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 3 of 5 stars

### My review

I saw this in a second-hand bookshop and, liking the blurb, decided to buy it.

*When I Forgot* is a dark contemporary story of only 180 pages.

It moves frequently between time periods and I found that confusing at times.

Yet the information being slowly revealed was intriguing enough to keep me reading.

Anna spends a day reflecting on her childhood and remembering a life filled with fear, distrust and uncertainty.

This story explores mental illness, the effects of war and the perspective of a child who does not fully understand what is happening around her.

The themes, emotions and hopelessness were easy to relate to.

Unfortunately, the writing style was not for me.

The confusion became distracting and eventually irritating.

It's a shame because beneath that style was a very moving story.
