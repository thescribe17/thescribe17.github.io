### Illusion by Paula Volsky

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 5 of 5 stars

### My review

Approximately fifteen years ago, I read *Illusion* and loved it.

This month I read it again and can see exactly why.

The book is over 700 pages long and any book requiring that amount of time and effort had better be worth it.

*Illusion* is.

Based loosely on the French Revolution, the story is set in a fantasy world centred on the city of Sherreen.

Eliste, a privileged young woman, travels to the city to be presented at court. We see exactly who she is and how she thinks.

Then everything changes.

Her life is thrown into turmoil and we travel with her from privilege and comfort to hunger, fear and survival.

There are more twists in this book than seem possible.

Each time a problem is overcome, something worse appears.

We witness not only changes in Eliste's circumstances, but changes in her personality and outlook.

The writing style reflects this transformation.

The story is filled with suspense, romance, politics, violence and intrigue.

The characters are convincing. The setting is rich and believable. The emotions linger long after the book is closed.

Even knowing what would happen, I still couldn't wait to return to Sherreen.

The outcome is brilliant.

This remains my number one book.

Highly recommended.
