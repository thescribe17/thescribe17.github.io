<p>Becoming an editor and/or proofreader means you must have an excellent understanding of grammar, spelling, punctuation, style, formatting and layout.</p>

<p>Obviously, no one can remember everything connected with these things so it is important to have a good set of tools.</p>

<p>Tools required are:</p>

<p>1. A good localised dictionary (ie it's no use owning a lovely thick American dictionary if you reside in the UK or Australia).<br>
2. A marking pen (ie standards change from company to company, so be sure you know what colour is used for each position, such as editor, copyeditor, proofreading or publisher).<br>
3. A localised style manual.</p>

<p>The above are the absolute minimum you must have.</p>

<p>Australians might considered subscribing to the <a href="http://www.macquariedictionary.com.au">Macquarie Dictionery Online</a>. However, there is an annual fee to access the information.</p>
