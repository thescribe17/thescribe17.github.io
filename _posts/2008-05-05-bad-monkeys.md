### Bad Monkeys by Matt Ruff

**Format:** Book

**My rating:** 4 of 5 stars

### My review

Wow!

I finished this book in two days.

That should say everything.

*Bad Monkeys* wasn't a book I would have picked up myself. Someone else placed it in my hands.

At first glance, the cover did nothing for me.

Then I read the first chapter.

Two hours later I had read over one hundred pages.

The author planted questions in my mind and constantly supplied new mysteries before answering the old ones.

I couldn't stop thinking about the story.

It's filled with twists and turns that leave you questioning what is real.

The characters are engaging and memorable, and the author's sense of humour makes the experience even more enjoyable.

The library classified it as a detective novel, but it blends mystery, science fiction, humour and something delightfully strange.

Do yourself a favour.

Read the book.
